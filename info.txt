Seed Database:

Assign Permissions to Admin Role via Tinker:
php artisan tinker

Inside Tinker:
use Spatie\Permission\Models\Role;
use Spatie\Permission\Models\Permission;

$admin = Role::where('name', 'Admin')->first();
$admin->syncPermissions(Permission::all());
$admin->refresh();
$admin->permissions->pluck('name');

$u = \App\Models\User::find(1);
$u->assignRole('Admin');
$u->refresh();
$u->getAllPermissions()->pluck('name');

Notes:

syncPermissions(Permission::all()) assigns all permissions to Admin.

pluck('name') is used to verify permissions.

Ensure spatie/laravel-permission package is installed and migrated.
